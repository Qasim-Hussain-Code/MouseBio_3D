# MVN1 cortical microvascular source

The sampled paths and retained topology metadata derive from Schmid F, Conti G, Jenny P and Weber B. Time-averaged simulations results for bi-phasic blood flow simulations in realistic microvascular networks for various single- and multi-capillary occlusion scenarios. Zenodo, 2021, version 1. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.5115640](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5115640). The deposit declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Original cortical geometry: Blinder P, Tsai PS, Kaufhold JP, Knutsen PM, Suhl H and Kleinfeld D. The cortical angiome: an interconnected vascular network with noncolumnar patterns of blood flow. Nature Neuroscience. 2013;16:889-897. [doi:10.1038/nn.3426](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3426).

Model methods: Schmid F, Tsai PS, Kleinfeld D, Jenny P and Weber B. Depth-dependent flow and pressure characteristics in cortical microvascular networks. PLoS Computational Biology. 2017;13:e1005392. [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005392](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005392). Related study: Schmid F, Conti G, Jenny P and Weber B. The severity of microstrokes depends on local vascular topology and baseline perfusion. eLife. 2021;10:e60208. [doi:10.7554/eLife.60208](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.60208).

Geometry-only adaptation by Qasim Hussain for MouseBio_3D, CC BY 4.0. Every sampled path and source topology identifier is retained. Serialized callables and simulation scripts were not executed. Coordinates are unchanged; their micrometre units are source-declared without independent physical calibration. No registration or anatomical overlay is supplied.

Simulated flow, pressure, hematocrit, effective diameters, adjusted vessel categories and path-analysis fields are omitted. No diameter sets line thickness, no measured lumen surface or physiological motion is generated. Edge-local endpoint records remain separate. All zero-length segments, path ordering differences and numerical source discrepancies are retained without repair. Individual specimen identity and exact characteristics remain unresolved.
