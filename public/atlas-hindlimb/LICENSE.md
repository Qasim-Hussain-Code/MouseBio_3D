# Charles et al. adult mouse hindlimb source notice

The regional surfaces in this directory are adapted from S1 Figure accompanying:

Charles JP, Cappellari O, Spence AJ, Hutchinson JR, Wells DJ. Musculoskeletal Geometry, Muscle Architecture and Functional Specialisations of the Mouse Hindlimb. *PLOS ONE*. 2016;11(4):e0147669. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0147669](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0147669). [S1 Figure: doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0147669.s002](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0147669.s002).

The source and these adapted surfaces are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source was published by PLOS; see its [license and copyright policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/licenses-and-copyright).

Modifications by Qasim Hussain for MouseBio_3D: extraction of the source PRC triangle surfaces, uniform conversion of the declared millimetre unit to metres, normalization of existing normal vectors, retention of source colours and identifiers, and serialization as glTF. No new geometry, smoothing, mesh repair, decimation or anatomical registration has been added.

The standalone PRC decoding tool used during preparation is a separate third-party tool distributed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. It is not bundled or linked into the MIT application. See [hindlimb geometry methods](../../docs/hindlimb-geometry.md).
