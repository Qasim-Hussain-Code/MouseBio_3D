# Development status

MouseBio_3D is a local scientific exploration prototype. Qasim Hussain is the sole owner and maintainer. Implementation details and scientific limits are recorded in the methods documents below. Repository visibility is controlled by Qasim Hussain.

## Implemented representation

The React, TypeScript and Three.js application provides seven bundled anatomical references and an optional browser-local SWC trace across four exploration modes. Two Digimouse models derive from one postmortem 28 g normal nude male mouse. A regional hindlimb source describes one adult female C57BL/6 mouse. EMA147 provides developmental TS23 surfaces with unresolved age estimates and unknown physical mesh units. Two further independent line references preserve regional mouse cortical microvascular sampled paths and a source-declared predicted regional nerve graph. The independent NeuroMorpho.Org NMO_130632 reference retains a source-declared adult mouse V1 layer 4 neuronal tracing as numeric samples and parent-linked lines. The architecture includes 14 system categories; missing coverage and unreviewed classifications remain explicit.

Each reference preserves its source IDs, attribution and coordinate frame. No accepted registration connects the bundled frames or a local trace to another reference. Numerical labels or bounding boxes do not establish correspondence. Experimental observations remain in separate in-memory banks when switching references. Optional finer surfaces retain the volume base frame and load selectively; Compare uses the same base surfaces in both panels.

The volume source has 0.1 mm isotropic sample spacing; subsampling does not establish anatomical accuracy. Hindlimb PRC units convert uniformly from millimetres to metres without independent calibration. Its figure has 37 muscle-named surfaces while the article reports 39 muscles; the discrepancy remains unresolved. Foot, Tibia/Fibula and Fabellae retain aggregate labels. See [volume methods](volume-geometry.md) and [hindlimb methods](hindlimb-geometry.md).

EMA147 preserves every source triangle and winding in native coordinates. Only 68 surface EMAPA identifiers match the separate anatomy index; eight occur only in each inventory. The reference surface has no asserted anatomical identity. Only 23 of 77 surfaces meet the executed closed-topology checks; defects remain present. Image voxel spacing is not applied to mesh coordinates. TS23 is retained with separately attributed E15.0, 15 dpc and 14.5 dpc claims. Sex, mass and explicit specimen count remain unknown. See [EMA147 methods](ema147-geometry.md).

NMO_130632 retains every numeric source record and parent link in its own unknown-unit frame, with original and derivative checksums and CC BY 4.0 attribution. The distributed derivative omits original comment headers containing contact and machine information. Individual sex, exact age, strain, mass and explicit specimen count remain unresolved; the source's sex category and age range are not individual measurements. Reported 15 percent shrinkage remains uncorrected, and the original-to-standardized transform is unverified. The source trace inspector reports graph counts and initial Float32 diagnostics without assigning physical accuracy, measured radius, cell counts, synapses or physiology. See [neuronal morphology methods](neuronal-morphology.md).

The local SWC reader retains original numeric sample IDs, type codes, coordinates, radii and parent IDs, while excluding filenames and comment text from the parsed asset. It hashes a copy of the exact original bytes, including ignored comments and line endings. Unknown physical units, source license and biological identity remain explicit. Radius fields do not set line thickness, and no cell surface, synapse or physiological model is generated. One local geometry asset is cached; reopening an earlier file with identical bytes restores its source-specific observations during the same browser session, subject to the bank capacity. See [local morphology methods](local-morphology.md).

The MVN1 source retains declared micrometres at numeric scale 1, with calibration and axes unverified. Edge-local points remain separate and original topology identities are preserved alongside rendering indices. One original graph component and cycle rank 2,904 differ from the 12,071 indexed path components. All 62 zero-length segments remain. Effective diameters, simulation fields and model categories are omitted. No blood flow or vessel surface is generated. See [vascular source methods](vascular-source-assessment.md).

The MouseMapper patch retains unknown units and every source point and ordered path. Source radius fields have incompatible tuple counts and are omitted without an inferred association. The example is predicted regional nerve geometry; exact anatomical subregion, individual specimen, sex, age, diet and laterality remain unresolved. Its 64 numeric components do not count nerves or cells. See [nerve source methods](nerve-source-assessment.md).

## Controls and interpretation

**Find anatomy** opens source choices after choosing a topic and collapses them after a route request, retaining space for the model. It routes blood vessels, nerves, brain, muscles, bones, tissue layers and the independent neuron trace to exact reviewed source selections. It can fade or hide other regions within the selected source and frames the existing geometry. A cross-reference request validates the target before adoption; failed reads retain the current view and observations. Coverage is regional, developmental, aggregate or single-record as stated by each source. References are never overlaid or registered by this operation.

Anatomy supports rotation, zoom, pan, selection, search, labels, visibility, isolation, opacity, display-axis clipping, reset and focus. Dark and White control the viewer background. Organs adjusts existing envelope and skeleton opacity. Structure details focuses the selected-region inspector. Source groups isolate existing regions and frame their bounds while retaining camera direction and roll. EMA147 groups select labelled surfaces, three vessel-labelled surfaces, two trigeminal-labelled surfaces or the unannotated reference. Its system classifications remain unreviewed. Additional narrow EMA147 selections expose named surfaces without skin/reference, neural-labelled surfaces, explicit bone/cartilage labels and explicit tissue/layer labels. The selected native-surface record exposes exact source identity, index-match status, vertex and triangle counts and expandable source topology diagnostics without physical measurements. No group supplies new anatomy, connectivity or registration.

Source layer composition combines available source groups, deduplicates overlapping IDs and hides or fades other regions. It retains observations and background, clears clipping and optional finer surfaces, and frames the selected bounds. Saved views preserve its resulting display settings. See [source layer methods](source-layers.md).

Living provides separate illustrative cardiac and respiratory deformations for Digimouse only. Periods and amplitudes are arbitrary display parameters. Hindlimb, EMA147, NMO_130632 and local traces have no implemented motion. Experiment and Compare validate local observations, calculate descriptive summaries and compare compatible conditions on the same reference with a common scale. User-declared provenance survives normalized JSON exports; it is not independently verified.

View files preserve display settings, background and an optional validated camera pose, bound to atlas ID, asset checksum and coordinate-frame ID. Supported older files without background default to Dark. Rejected imports leave current settings intact, and observations remain separate. A local-trace view contains no sample rows; reopen the identical original file before restoring it. The tetrahedral reference's proposed MA associations retain their computational-inference status and do not transfer to other references.

Source sample explorer supports exact-ID lookup, source-row navigation, parent and bounded child inspection in bundled and local traces. Numerical graph categories retain no cell or connectivity claim. Its focus operation frames existing visible, unclipped incident links while retaining camera direction and roll. Inspector selection is transient and absent from saved views and observation exports. See [sample inspection](trace-sample-inspection.md).

Source path explorer reads the checked regional nerve or vascular derivative after an explicit request, with reuse of a still-live parser-owned asset when available. It supports exact source path IDs, source-ordered navigation, bounded point pages and the vascular source's explicit endpoint and incident-path records. Focus source path frames retained existing segments while preserving camera direction and roll. Inspection stays separate from experimental IDs and exports; saved views may retain the resulting camera pose without graph records. See [path inspection methods](source-path-inspection.md).

## Verification and remaining work

Executed checks and their limits are recorded in [acceptance criteria](acceptance.md). Release records describe their corresponding source snapshots. The source-path integration passed 294 JavaScript and simulated DOM cases, all 111 Python cases with retained originals, dependency notices, TypeScript, the production build and all nine loopback HTTP checks. Actual-source inspection cases cover every nerve path point page, all vascular paths and explicit topology vertices. Focus checks cover source rows, world transforms, recentering, clipping planes, draw range, hidden or coincident geometry, malformed buffers and unchanged source records. Simulated workflows cover explicit reads, zero-based IDs, bounded pages, endpoint navigation, cancellation, fixed errors, camera-focus callbacks, white background and unchanged observations and saved-view records. Resource accounting remains below the 10,000,000,000-byte project cap. See [v0.12.0 release notes](releases/v0.12.0.md). Numerical geometry checks, actual Three.js loaders and simulated DOM tests do not establish browser pixels, pointer interactions, representative GPU performance or independent anatomical accuracy. A user-provided screenshot supports only its shown views. An initial Chrome workspace view was observed, but reliable browser automation subsequently stopped because the runtime could not determine the current URL. The regional graphs and path-inspection controls remain visually unverified; the current browser access host is unavailable. Systematic browser interaction, memory and frame-rate checks remain incomplete.

Native anatomical directions and physical calibration remain independently unverified. EMA147 and neuronal trace units are unknown. Display-axis clipping is not histological section reconstruction, and magnification adds no biological resolution. Skeleton remains aggregate in Digimouse; developmental vessel and nerve-labelled surfaces do not establish complete adult vascular or peripheral-neural anatomy. Limited source-labelled developmental tissue and layer surfaces are present. Female whole-body, population-average and disease-specific anatomy and microscopic histological reconstruction remain unavailable. A source-declared measured neuronal tracing is bundled as an independent line reference. Its source declarations and parent graph do not establish independently verified cell identity, a reconstructed cell surface, complete cellular coverage or registration to another reference. No comprehensive connectivity or quantitative physiological model is implemented.

Generated GLB and PLY assets occupy 89,102,891 bytes. Numeric neuronal and polyline derivatives add 38,836,114 bytes, for 127,939,005 bytes across 28 bundled scientific assets before manifests and notices. The project cap is 10,000,000,000 bytes including dependencies, caches, archives, intermediates, release artifacts and Git metadata. Full CT, PET and cryosection collections have not been acquired. Linux Mint with 16 GB RAM and integrated graphics remains a target rather than an established benchmark.

Further work requires systematic browser acceptance, hardware measurement, reviewed anatomical orientation and accepted registration before any source combination. Additional anatomy needs documented, reusable sources and explicit coverage. Quantitative physiology requires equations, parameters, units, uncertainty and independent validation.
