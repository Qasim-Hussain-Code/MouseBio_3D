# Development status

MouseBio_3D is a local scientific exploration prototype. Qasim Hussain is the sole owner and maintainer. Implementation details and scientific limits are recorded in the methods documents below. The repository is public by the decision of Qasim Hussain, who controls its visibility.

## Implemented representation

The React, TypeScript and Three.js application provides eight bundled anatomical references and an optional browser-local SWC trace across four exploration modes. Two Digimouse models derive from one postmortem 28 g normal nude male mouse. A micro-CT reference adds the body and ten organ classes, including the intestine and bone, from one native in vivo scan of a female BALB/c nu/nu mouse. A regional hindlimb source describes one adult female C57BL/6 mouse. EMA147 provides developmental TS23 surfaces with unresolved age estimates and unknown physical mesh units. Two further independent line references preserve regional mouse cortical microvascular sampled paths and a source-declared predicted regional nerve graph. The independent NeuroMorpho.Org NMO_130632 reference retains a source-declared adult mouse V1 layer 4 neuronal tracing as numeric samples and parent-linked lines. The architecture includes 14 system categories; missing coverage and unreviewed classifications remain explicit.

Each reference preserves its source IDs, attribution and coordinate frame. No accepted registration connects the bundled frames or a local trace to another reference. Numerical labels or bounding boxes do not establish correspondence. Experimental observations remain in separate in-memory banks when switching references. Optional finer surfaces retain the volume base frame and load selectively; Compare uses the same base surfaces in both panels.

The volume source has 0.1 mm isotropic sample spacing; subsampling does not establish anatomical accuracy. Hindlimb PRC units convert uniformly from millimetres to metres without independent calibration. Its figure has 37 muscle-named surfaces while the article reports 39 muscles; the discrepancy remains unresolved. Foot, Tibia/Fibula and Fabellae retain aggregate labels. See [volume methods](volume-geometry.md), [micro-CT methods](microct-geometry.md) and [hindlimb methods](hindlimb-geometry.md).

EMA147 preserves every source triangle and winding in native coordinates. Only 68 surface EMAPA identifiers match the separate anatomy index; eight occur only in each inventory. The reference surface has no asserted anatomical identity. Only 23 of 77 surfaces meet the executed closed-topology checks; defects remain present. Image voxel spacing is not applied to mesh coordinates. TS23 is retained with separately attributed E15.0, 15 dpc and 14.5 dpc claims. Sex, mass and explicit specimen count remain unknown. See [EMA147 methods](ema147-geometry.md).

NMO_130632 retains every numeric source record and parent link in its own unknown-unit frame, with original and derivative checksums and CC BY 4.0 attribution. The distributed derivative omits original comment headers containing contact and machine information. Individual sex, exact age, strain, mass and explicit specimen count remain unresolved; the source's sex category and age range are not individual measurements. Reported 15 percent shrinkage remains uncorrected, and the original-to-standardized transform is unverified. The source trace inspector reports graph counts and initial Float32 diagnostics without assigning physical accuracy, measured radius, cell counts, synapses or physiology. See [neuronal morphology methods](neuronal-morphology.md).

The local SWC reader retains original numeric sample IDs, type codes, coordinates, radii and parent IDs, while excluding filenames and comment text from the parsed asset. It hashes a copy of the exact original bytes, including ignored comments and line endings. Unknown physical units, source license and biological identity remain explicit. Radius fields do not set line thickness, and no cell surface, synapse or physiological model is generated. One local geometry asset is cached; reopening an earlier file with identical bytes restores its source-specific observations during the same browser session, subject to the bank capacity. See [local morphology methods](local-morphology.md).

The MVN1 source retains declared micrometres at numeric scale 1, with calibration and axes unverified. Edge-local points remain separate and original topology identities are preserved alongside rendering indices. One original graph component and cycle rank 2,904 differ from the 12,071 indexed path components. All 62 zero-length segments remain. Effective diameters, simulation fields and model categories are omitted. No blood flow or vessel surface is generated. See [vascular source methods](vascular-source-assessment.md).

The MouseMapper patch retains unknown units and every source point and ordered path. Source radius fields have incompatible tuple counts and are omitted without an inferred association. The example is predicted regional nerve geometry; exact anatomical subregion, individual specimen, sex, age, diet and laterality remain unresolved. Its 64 numeric components do not count nerves or cells. See [nerve source methods](nerve-source-assessment.md).

## Controls and interpretation

**Find anatomy** opens source choices after choosing a topic and collapses them after a route request, retaining space for the model. It routes blood vessels, nerves, brain, muscles, bones, tissue layers and the independent neuron trace to exact reviewed source selections. It can fade or hide other regions within the selected source and frames the existing geometry. A cross-reference request validates the target before adoption; failed reads retain the current view and observations. Coverage is regional, developmental, aggregate or single-record as stated by each source. References are never overlaid or registered by this operation.

Anatomy supports rotation, zoom, pan, selection, search, labels, visibility, isolation, opacity, display-axis clipping, reset and focus. The viewer has a single white background. The Digimouse references open with the body envelope as opaque skin, a **Skin** opacity control, a display posture with the dorsal side up inferred from source label positions and a ground shadow. It applies image-based lighting, neutral tone mapping, a view-dependent translucent envelope, a selection rim light and optional automatic rotation, shows voxel-derived Digimouse surfaces as display-only Taubin-smoothed copies with a source-surface option, and colours regions by a tissue-like display scheme with a manifest-colour option; these are presentation conventions recorded in [viewer presentation methods](viewer-presentation.md). Organs adjusts existing envelope and skeleton opacity. Structure details focuses the selected-region inspector. Source groups isolate existing regions and frame their bounds while retaining camera direction and roll. EMA147 groups select labelled surfaces, three vessel-labelled surfaces, two trigeminal-labelled surfaces or the unannotated reference. Its system classifications remain unreviewed. Additional narrow EMA147 selections expose named surfaces without skin/reference, neural-labelled surfaces, explicit bone/cartilage labels and explicit tissue/layer labels. The selected native-surface record exposes exact source identity, index-match status, vertex and triangle counts and expandable source topology diagnostics without physical measurements. No group supplies new anatomy, connectivity or registration.

Source layer composition combines available source groups, deduplicates overlapping IDs and hides or fades other regions. It retains observations and background, clears clipping and optional finer surfaces, and frames the selected bounds. Saved views preserve its resulting display settings. See [source layer methods](source-layers.md).

Start here offers eight guided source views and an independent cardiac-cell entry point. Each view validates its exact source and existing group before adoption. An explicitly requested fictional comparison walkthrough is available only when it would not displace accepted observations or an active import. Synthetic provenance persists through normal JSON exports.

The workbench places the viewer centrally, with a navigator of Structures, Layers and Sources tabs, a selection toolbar, a camera pad with keyboard navigation and a tabbed details inspector that opens by default on wide screens. System layers are derived only from source classifications; unrepresented categories are listed without controls. Selecting a hidden or transparent region makes it visible. Native-unit and source-graph calibration notices remain visible.

Living provides separate illustrative cardiac and respiratory deformations for Digimouse only. Periods and amplitudes are arbitrary display parameters. Hindlimb, EMA147, NMO_130632 and local traces have no implemented surface motion. For both Digimouse references, schematic routes animate blood tracers through pulmonary, systemic and hepatic portal circuits and pulses along vagal, trigeminal motor and optic routes. The route table holds 26 organ-level educational simplifications citing OpenStax *Anatomy and Physiology 2e*; it resolves to 30 tetrahedral and 26 volume connectors, with three tetrahedral routes omitted for unsegmented organs. Connectors join existing source-surface anchors and are not vessel or nerve geometry. Nucleus-level origins assigned to undivided labels are recorded as inferences. Timing is arbitrary and shares the illustrative clock. See [schematic route methods](living-circuits.md). On the regional MVN1 reference, an assumed-parameter Hagen-Poiseuille calculation drives tracers along one retained path. On the MouseMapper reference, a generic dimensionless FitzHugh-Nagumo cable is displayed along one path by normalized length. Neither regional model is calibrated or validated. See [path flow methods](path-flow-model.md) and [excitable cable methods](excitable-cable-model.md). A separate 41-state Bondarenko apical ventricular-cell model computes membrane voltage and calcium handling at the source temperature of 298 K. Its archived-source and explicitly modified balanced-transition variants remain independent of anatomical views and observation banks. The archived transition defect, optional mathematical correction and absence of independent physiological validation are disclosed before calculation. See [cardiac cell methods](cardiac-cell-model.md). Experiment and Compare validate local observations, calculate descriptive summaries and compare compatible conditions on the same reference with a common scale. User-declared provenance survives normalized JSON exports; it is not independently verified.

View files preserve display settings, background and an optional validated camera pose, bound to atlas ID, asset checksum and coordinate-frame ID. Older files that record the retired dark background, or none, load with the white background. Rejected imports leave current settings intact, and observations remain separate. A local-trace view contains no sample rows; reopen the identical original file before restoring it. The tetrahedral reference's proposed MA associations retain their computational-inference status and do not transfer to other references.

Source sample explorer supports exact-ID lookup, source-row navigation, parent and bounded child inspection in bundled and local traces. Numerical graph categories retain no cell or connectivity claim. Its focus operation frames existing visible, unclipped incident links while retaining camera direction and roll. Inspector selection is transient and absent from saved views and observation exports. See [sample inspection](trace-sample-inspection.md).

Source path explorer reads the checked regional nerve or vascular derivative after an explicit request, with reuse of a still-live parser-owned asset when available. It supports exact source path IDs, source-ordered navigation, bounded point pages and the vascular source's explicit endpoint and incident-path records. Focus source path frames retained existing segments while preserving camera direction and roll. Inspection stays separate from experimental IDs and exports; saved views may retain the resulting camera pose without graph records. See [path inspection methods](source-path-inspection.md).

## One unbroken zoom, a moving mouse and a plain view

Scrolling opens the living mouse at the spot under the pointer, layer by layer in the order in which the structures lie there, and goes on in the same place on the screen through the tissue of the structure under the pointer to atoms, by way of a cell nucleus, a red cell and mouse hemoglobin, or the bacteria of the gut. All 385 structures have a route, by way of thirty kinds of tissue. The mouse moves and answers the pointer. All of this is illustrative or simulated and is marked so. The working view has been cleared of explanations, which remain in the inspector, **Sources** and the documents, and simulated structures keep a short mark on the view. See [zooming through the layers](zoom-unfold.md), [the zoom below the layers](scale-journey.md) and [illustrative motion](living-motion.md).

Planned work includes a measured molecule for each tissue, a route of its own for each structure now served by its nearest kind of tissue, a stepping that bends the joints, and a measure of the smoothness of the motion on a graphics card.

## Verification and remaining work

Most tissues now have a way from their cells to a measured molecule of their own, through two simulated levels, and **Play** takes it. Sixteen Protein Data Bank entries supply the atoms, nine of them not wholly of the mouse, and each such level says so. The walk bends its legs at a middle joint. See [the zoom below the layers](scale-journey.md#a-molecule-for-each-tissue).

Skeleton remains aggregate in Digimouse; developmental vessel and nerve-labelled surfaces do not establish complete adult vascular or peripheral-neural anatomy. Comprehensive connectivity and whole-organism physiology remain unavailable; the schematic routes are organ-level teaching connectors, not connectivity data. The independent parameterized cardiac-cell model does not establish physiological validation or supply cellular geometry at anatomical points.
