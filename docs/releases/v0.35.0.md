# v0.35.0 the inner ear and small parts of the head

The zoom gains a route of its own into the inner ear, and the living mouse model gains six simulated structures, and now holds 391. The source references are unchanged. Everything added to the model is simulated from published descriptions and is marked as simulated wherever it appears.

## Implemented behavior

- **A route into the inner ear:** the cochlea cut across, then the organ of Corti with its inner and outer hair cells, after Schwander et al. 2010 ([doi:10.1083/jcb.201001138](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.201001138)).
- **A way to the mouse tip link:** a bundle of stereocilia, one tip link, and then the measured bond of cadherin 23 and protocadherin 15 ([4AQ8](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4AQ8)), with the atoms where they meet. After Kazmierczak et al. 2007 ([doi:10.1038/nature06091](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06091)) and Sotomayor et al. 2012 ([doi:10.1038/nature11590](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11590)). **Play** from the inner ear takes this way. Calcium has its own colour and size in the atoms.
- **Whisker follicles** under the mystacial whiskers, after Ebara et al. 2002 ([doi:10.1002/cne.10277](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.10277)), who describe them in the rat and the cat.
- **Dural venous sinuses** and **meningeal lymphatic vessels** beside them, after Louveau et al. 2015 ([doi:10.1038/nature14432](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14432)), who found these lymphatic vessels in mice. Both carry tokens of flow.
- **The vomer, ethmoid and lacrimal bones**, which the CT does not separate, after Greene 1935, who describes the rat.

## Limits

- The two levels of the inner ear and the two of the way on are simulated illustrations. Their sizes, counts and positions are estimates, and the canals of balance are not drawn.
- The tip link in the crystal is two repeats of each protein, far shorter than the whole link. It was made in the laboratory and was not measured in this mouse.
- None of the new structures is seen in the source images. No path follows a real one, and only the sagittal and transverse sinuses are drawn.
- No anatomist has reviewed them. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
