# v0.25.0 fibulae, middle ears and a list of coverage

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 291 structures: 36 source-labelled, 152 traced and 103 simulated. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- The tibia and fibula are traced apart from the CT. Seeds placed on each bone in sections of the CT grow through bone, and each voxel goes to the nearer seed along a path through bone. The free part of each fibula becomes a separate bone, and the distal part, where the CT shows the two as one, stays with the tibia. Hummel et al. describe the two as separate for the proximal two thirds and fused along the distal third. The limits between them are estimates.
- New simulated structures are the patellae and medial fabellae, the ear canals, tympanic membranes and ossicles, the pineal gland, the parathyroid glands, the adrenal medullae and the internal mammary arteries and veins.
- The [coverage list](../living-mouse-coverage.md) records what the model holds for each body system, and what it leaves out.

## Evidence for placement

The ear canals and tympanic membranes lie in the tympanic bullae and the pineal gland lies on the dorsal surface of the brain, as compared on colour sections of the specimen. The first version of the pineal gland reached into the skull in those sections, and it was lowered. The internal mammary vessels lie inside the chest wall beside the sternum in the same sections. Each parathyroid gland lies on the dorsolateral border of a simulated thyroid lobe. Each patella lies on the front of a knee, with its vertices less than a millimetre from the femur and tibia on average.

Their sizes and their exact courses are estimates. The ossicles are too small to be seen in the CT at this resolution, so their shapes and places follow descriptions alone.

## Limits

These structures are simulated, marked as such, and have not been compared with any dissection. The patellae were not located in the CT: bright straight marks at the knees proved to be alignment rods outside the body, and so were not used.
