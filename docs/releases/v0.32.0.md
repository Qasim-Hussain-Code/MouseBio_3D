# v0.32.0 a molecule for each tissue, and a walk with joints

Each tissue can now go on from its cells to a measured molecule of its own, **Play** takes that way, and the mouse walks with bending legs. The anatomy of the living mouse model is unchanged: the GLB is byte-identical to that of v0.31.0, with 377 structures. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- [A molecule for each tissue](../scale-journey.md). Sixteen ways, each through two simulated levels of the inside of a cell, reach the atoms of a Protein Data Bank entry, shown whole and then round one chosen atom, and go on to a carbon atom and the proton. They are listed in that document with their species, method and sources.
- **Play** from a tissue takes the way of that tissue, and from a tissue with none it goes to a cell nucleus. It goes on to the proton in one press from the closed mouse or from any level.
- A list under the levels of the zoom names the ways on from the level in view, so that a way can be chosen when its place is out of view, as well as by pointing.
- Atoms are drawn as round balls even where a very large molecule uses few faces, and complex I, the largest, has a wider view of its own so that it can be seen whole.
- [The walk](../living-motion.md). A foot now comes forward through the air with its leg bent and goes back along the ground, which earlier versions had the wrong way round. The trunk curves a little with the stride.
- Play no longer stalls if the view has drifted off the animal when it is pressed, and its pace no longer depends on the frame rate.

## Limits

- The molecules were not measured in this mouse, and nine of the sixteen are not wholly of the mouse. Each such level says so. A synthetic peptide stands in for collagen.
- The levels between the cells and the molecules are simulated from published descriptions, and their sizes, counts and positions are estimates.
- The walk is a sketch: its middle joint lies on a straight line, the other joints do not move, and its pace is chosen for viewing.
- No expert has reviewed these levels, and the pictures come from software rendering. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
