# v0.18.0 hair, named muscles and organ parts

This release deepens the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md). The coat is now drawn as individual hairs, the traced muscle tissue is divided into named muscles, organs are divided into named parts, and a new control peels the model from the coat inwards. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- The coat is drawn as about 280,000 individual hairs, each a tapered ribbon with a dark root, pale band and dark tip, instead of stacked shells, which showed visible rings up close. The skin under the coat takes the colour of the undercoat.
- **From the outside in** in Layers chooses Coat, Skin, Fat and muscle, Muscles, Organs, Blood vessels, Nerves or Skeleton with one click. Each step only chooses which existing structures are shown.
- The traced muscle tissue is divided into 41 named muscles, with fine stripes along the direction of each and pale patches at its ends. Hind limb names follow Charles et al. (2016), and other names follow a description of the rat.
- The heart is divided into four chambers, the right lung into four lobes, the liver into four lobes and the stomach into two regions, as named by Cook. The kidneys and testes are separated by side. The traced gut is divided into small intestine, caecum, and colon and rectum.
- A new mark, "division estimated", states that the outer form follows measured tissue but that the boundaries and name of the part are estimates. **Show by evidence** treats these parts by the evidence for their tissue, so the measured model can still be seen whole with simulated structures hidden.
- Structures of the trunk and head no longer follow a limb that lay beside them in the scan, which had stretched a jugular vein towards the forearm. The ears are now thin sheets with a smooth rim.

## Executed verification

449 JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips, including new cases for the layer stack, the division marks and the scattering of hairs, which repeats exactly and leaves bare skin without hair. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The build reproduced the model byte for byte. A test that checks every tracked path against the release packager was added in v0.17.1.

A headless Chrome session with software WebGL rendered the model from the front, side, rear and close up, each layer step, and Living, Experiment and Compare on this reference, without console errors. Screenshots were inspected visually. This is software rendering, not a measurement of performance on a real GPU, and it is not anatomical review.

## Scientific and operational limits

Muscle and organ boundaries are estimates placed by hand and do not follow fascia or fissures. Internal cavities of the heart are not modelled. Hairs are far fewer and wider than in a real coat. All limits of [v0.17.0](v0.17.0.md) still apply. The model holds about 2.4 million triangles in 68 MB, and all of them are drawn only when the skin is removed.
