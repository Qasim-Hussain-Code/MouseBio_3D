# v0.41.1 plainer notes of evidence and the license in words

A small release that changes wording on the screen and removes one control that did not work on every device. The model and its geometry are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- **Traced structures:** the details of a structure traced from the source images now say that it was traced from them, and the descriptions of the traced tissues no longer describe the means of tracing. The line under the view names the kind of model and nothing more.
- **Sources and evidence:** the page no longer carries a list of checks that the application cannot make, or a note beside the citation of the description of the rat. The procedures are still described as no replacement for supervised training or an approved animal protocol.
- **License:** the page names the license of the anatomical model, CC BY-SA 4.0, and says what it asks of anyone who reuses the model: they may use and adapt it, crediting its authors, and anything adapted from it must keep the same license. An empty field of attribution is no longer shown as "Unknown".
- **Tutorials:** the button that read the steps aloud is removed. It used only a voice installed on the device, and where none was installed it said nothing.

## Limits

- The model and its limits are unchanged. They are recorded in [living mouse methods](../living-mouse.md) and [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
