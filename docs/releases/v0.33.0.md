# v0.33.0 joints, marrow and small parts

The living mouse model gains eight simulated structures, and now holds 385. The source references are unchanged. Everything added here is simulated from published descriptions and is marked as simulated wherever it appears.

## Implemented behavior

- **Articular cartilage** at each hip, knee, ankle, shoulder, elbow and wrist, and **menisci** at each knee, after Oliviero et al. 2023 ([doi:10.3389/fendo.2022.1016321](https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2022.1016321)).
- **Patellar tendons**, after Dyment et al. 2013 ([doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0059944](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0059944)).
- **Marrow** inside the shafts of the femur, tibia and humerus, placed on the centre line of each traced bone and under half of its measured thickness, after Jilka 2013 ([doi:10.1093/gerona/glt046](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glt046)).
- **Peyer's patches** on the small intestine, after Sauter et al. 2014 ([doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0105429](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0105429)). Nine are drawn, an estimate.
- An **enteric nerve plexus**, a net on the small intestine, caecum and colon, after Furness 2012 ([doi:10.1038/nrgastro.2012.32](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrgastro.2012.32)). The real net lies in the wall of the gut in two layers, which are not divided here.
- **Vomeronasal organs** low on each side of the nasal septum, under the floor of the simulated nasal cavity and over the premaxilla, after Kimoto et al. 2005 ([doi:10.1038/nature04033](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04033)).
- **Meibomian glands** in the upper and lower eyelids, after Mauris et al. 2015 ([doi:10.1038/cddis.2015.98](https://doi.org/10.1038/cddis.2015.98)).
- Each has a way down: cartilage to cartilage, tendon to tendon, marrow to blood cells, patches to lymphoid tissue, plexus to nerve, glands to gland, and the vomeronasal organ to the nearest route, the airway.
- Names of parts that would lie under the list of levels are left out, since the list would cover them.

## Limits

- None of these structures is seen in the source images. Their places and sizes are estimates, and no path follows a real one.
- The marrow is a core and does not hollow the bone, and the bone keeps its traced surface.
- No anatomist has reviewed them. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
