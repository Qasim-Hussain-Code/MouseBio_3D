# v0.38.0 walking, a voice and dissection with hands

The mouse now walks about the floor of the room, answers a touch with a voice, and sits up to wash. A new mode, **Dissect**, opens a bench where a pair of gloved hands uses instruments in real time on the layers of the model, with ten step-by-step tutorials. **Play** gives every level of the zoom a similar time. The coat has been rebuilt where a limb meets the trunk, and the mark and tab icon are now slow renderings of this whole mouse.

## Implemented behavior

- **Dissection practice:** a wax board, a tray of instruments and a dish, with the animal on its back or on its feet. The right hand follows the pointer and the left steadies the tissue with forceps. Eleven tools: a free hand, scalpel, scissors, forceps, blunt probe, bone cutters, ligature, needle and suture, pin, swab and syringe. Cuts are held in a grid in the coordinates of the model at rest, and everything under them is the geometry of the model. A structure can be lifted out into the dish and followed under the microscope into the zoom. Every action can be undone, and a session can be saved as a record. See [dissection practice](../dissection-lab.md).
- **Layers that draw back:** a long straight cut in the skin or the wall of the body draws a little apart. Forceps then draw each side back, and it folds over a line that moves out from the cut and lies out from the body with its inner side up. A cut that carries on along an earlier one opens with it as one. The front of the rib cage, once cut on both sides, lifts as one piece like a lid and turns over towards the head, and a cut along the top of the skull leaves a leaf of bone on each side, which folds out. Inner tissue has a wet shine under the lamp at the bench, and the fur is parted along a line marked for cutting.
- **The animal on its back:** the legs lie out to the sides, with the paws sole up. Only the part of a leg that hangs free turns far, and the trunk, the organs and the hip bones stay where they are, which removes the stretched tubes and the deformed rump of the first version of this pose. For surgery on the inner side of the thigh, that leg is drawn further out, as the published method tapes it down.
- **The wall of the abdomen:** the model had strips of traced muscle over the abdomen, with gaps through which the organs showed. A simulated sheet now closes the belly and flanks between the margin of the ribs and a line from the pubis towards the hip bone, with a pale middle line, after the mouse sections of Carlson and Chakkalakal 2011 ([doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0024212](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0024212)). It is drawn faintly everywhere except at the bench, where it is a layer to be opened. Simulated lateral veins in the tail, after Machholz et al. 2012, have been added too, and the model now holds 393 structures. The simulated femoral vein now lies beside the artery and on its inner side, so that the two can be told apart in a wound.
- **Tutorials:** ten, after the published outlines of Parkinson et al. 2011 ([doi:10.3791/2966](https://doi.org/10.3791/2966)), Niiyama et al. 2009 ([doi:10.3791/1035](https://doi.org/10.3791/1035)), Gage et al. 2012 ([doi:10.3791/3564](https://doi.org/10.3791/3564)) and Machholz et al. 2012 ([doi:10.3791/2771](https://doi.org/10.3791/2771)). A list offers them when the bench opens. A card says for each step what to do, why, what can go wrong and what is seen, takes up the instrument and the view, and marks the place on the animal: a dotted line that goes as the blade passes, a ring, a point on an edge, or a glow on a structure to find. It says what has fallen short, such as how long a cut is so far or how far it strayed. **Steady hand** keeps a stroke to a marked line, and stops a needle once its tip is in a named muscle. A needle goes in at the angle the method gives, and for an injection under the skin the left hand lifts a fold first. The views stand close enough to work in, with the animal in the part of the view that the card leaves free, and always from above the bench. The hands can show one step or the whole procedure, and the steps can be read aloud in a voice on the device.
- **Walking in the room:** the mouse goes from place to place, stops, walks out of the view and comes back later near any corner. A click on the floor sends it there. Its feet stay planted, because the distance it covers follows from its strides.
- **Washing:** standing, and at some stops, it sits up on its hind legs and brings its fore legs up to its face. Rodents do this often (Kalueff et al. 2016, [doi:10.1038/nrn.2015.8](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2015.8)). The movement itself is chosen for viewing and follows no measured sequence.
- **A voice:** a touch brings a short synthesized call, after the frequency bands in Grimsley et al. 2016 ([doi:10.3389/fnbeh.2016.00038](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbeh.2016.00038)), which can be silenced beside the view.
- **Even pace in Play:** each level of the zoom now takes a similar time, about eight seconds at the usual speed, with a pause and three speeds, so that the levels below the tissue no longer pass in a moment.
- **A whole coat:** a hair now turns with the skin it grows from, so that the fur stays whole when the head turns. Colour, length and direction of the coat now change smoothly over the skin, narrow folds between a limb and the trunk are filled, and the chest beside an arm stays pale, which removes dark streaks that showed between the fore legs and along the flank. The paws have more fine hair, running on from the fur of the limb.
- **The name and mark:** the mark beside the name is this whole mouse, rendered from the model with its coat: over twelve seconds it breathes, looks about, lifts its nose and takes two slow steps. The icon of the browser tab, seen from nearer the front so that the mouse fills the square, breathes and turns its head slowly. The name has been reset in a printed face.
- A button among the camera controls saves a picture of the view.

## Limits

- The dissection is practice on a simulated animal. It models no tissue mechanics, anaesthesia, aseptic technique or animal welfare, and it is not a protocol. It does not replace supervised training under an approved protocol. A layer that draws back is a sheet shaped for viewing, whose inner side is a drawn look with vessels from a fixed seed, and bleeding is an illustration with no volume or rate.
- The hands and instruments are drawings, with proportions estimated for display and ordinary ways of holding. An animal on its back is the standing model with its legs laid out by a display deformation, and the skin stretches in the groin.
- What a needle passes through is found from the surfaces of this one model. It says nothing about a real animal of another strain, size or fill of the gut.
- Filling the folds of the skin changes the shape of the simulated exterior behind the neck by up to about 2 mm in those folds. It is a choice for appearance. The traced and measured structures are not changed by it, except where their fit under the skin is relaxed.
- The walking, the washing and the voice are illustrations. The voice was not heard in testing, because the checks ran in a headless browser, and the animated tab icon cannot be seen there. The checks ran on one integrated graphics chip, at about 15 frames per second at the bench, with fewer hairs drawn to keep that rate. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
