# v0.23.0 insides of organs and measured corrections

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 282 structures: 36 source-labelled, 150 traced and 96 simulated. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- **Cut through the body** now passes through the middle of the body surface. It used the middle of the whole model, which the tail and limbs shift to one side, so it missed the head and back.
- Sections now show simulated insides of organs: the spaces of the heart with their valves, the renal medullae and pelves, the lumen and limiting ridge of the stomach, the lumen of the bladder, the ventricles of the brain, and the corneas, irises, lenses and retinas of the eyes.
- New simulated structures are the larynx with its named cartilages and epiglottis, tracheal rings, bile and pancreatic ducts, brown fat at the sites named by Hummel et al., bulbourethral and ampullary glands, Harderian and intraorbital lacrimal glands, the panniculus carnosus, the middle caudal vessels in the tail, the anus and preputial opening, and the eight cranial nerves not yet drawn, which completes all twelve pairs.
- Two small bones that the source skeleton label leaves out are traced from the CT and named by shape and position: the hyoid and the os penis.
- The application opens with the skin selected, and no label covers the animal when it is. Each structure of this model keeps the colour chosen for it, so that the ventricles of the brain are no longer drawn in the colour of the heart.

## Corrections

Placements were compared with colour sections of the specimen and with the CT. These parts of earlier versions were wrong and have moved:

- The penis ran straight back past the testes. The os penis in the CT tapers from its broad base at y 784 back to y 812, so the penis is now a hairpin whose glans turns back along that bone. The urethra and preputial glands moved with it.
- The teeth were placed too high and too far forward, and off the midline of the skull. The incisors and molars now lie where the CT shows them, and the tongue lies behind the lower incisors.
- The trachea, oesophagus and thyroid lay beside the midline of the neck, and the inner ears and some cranial nerves beside the midline of the head, because the midline of the trunk had been used for them. The larynx now lies behind the hyoid seen in the CT.
- The lenses were placed at the centre of the source eye label, which is not the centre of the simulated globe. They now lie inside the globe.

## Executed verification

All JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips, including new cases for the insides of organs, the small bones, the section position and the opening selection. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The distribution, publication and release suites passed. A rebuild from the raw volumes was byte-identical. A headless Chrome session with software WebGL rendered the opening view and sections through the head, chest and rump, without console errors. This is software rendering and not anatomical review.

## Scientific and operational limits

The source images do not separate the spaces inside organs, so their shapes follow the outline of a source label and their wall thicknesses are estimates. The readings of the sections and the CT were made by the author of this model and were not reviewed by an anatomist. The trachea is collapsed in this specimen, so its course is still an estimate. Preputial glands could not be identified in the sections. All limits of the earlier living mouse releases still apply.
