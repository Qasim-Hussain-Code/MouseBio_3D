# v0.34.0 a ribosome, thyroglobulin, a claudin and an immunoproteasome

The zoom below the layers gains four ways to measured molecules, so that twenty entries of the Protein Data Bank now supply its atoms. The source references and the anatomical model are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- **A whole mouse ribosome** ([7CPV](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7CPV)), 206,007 atoms from the testis of mice, reached from the testis and secreting glands, and from the endocrine glands if chosen, by rough reticulum and ribosomes on its membrane. The atoms shown next are those of RPL39L, the protein of male germ cells. After Li et al. 2022 ([doi:10.1038/s41586-022-05508-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05508-0)) and Palade 1975 ([doi:10.1126/science.1096303](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1096303)).
- **Thyroglobulin** ([7N4Y](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7N4Y)), naturally iodinated, of the cow, reached from the thyroid by way of its colloid, with the atoms of thyroxine and its iodine shown next. After Kim et al. 2021 ([doi:10.1107/S2059798321010056](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2059798321010056)).
- **A mouse claudin** ([4P79](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4P79)), reached from the lining of the bladder and ureters and the serous membranes, and from the small intestine if chosen, by the place where two cells meet and the strands of a tight junction. After Suzuki et al. 2014 ([doi:10.1126/science.1248571](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1248571)), Tamura et al. 2011 ([doi:10.1053/j.gastro.2010.08.006](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2010.08.006)), Acharya et al. 2004 ([doi:10.1152/ajprenal.00341.2003](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajprenal.00341.2003)) and Pitaraki et al. ([doi:10.1016/j.bbrc.2023.149376](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2023.149376)).
- **The mouse immunoproteasome** ([3UNH](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3UNH)), reached from lymphoid tissue if chosen, by the cytoplasm of a lymphocyte. After Huber et al. 2012 ([doi:10.1016/j.cell.2011.12.030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2011.12.030)).
- **Tooth to collagen**, by the dentine, after Goldberg et al. 2011 ([doi:10.2741/e281](https://doi.org/10.2741/e281)).
- **Play** from the thyroid, testis, glands, lining of the bladder, serous membranes and teeth takes these ways.
- **Tokens of blood and nerve** are drawn only in the vessels and nerves that are shown. They used to go on moving through empty space in layers that had taken those vessels out.
- Iodine and selenium have their own colours and sizes in the atoms, and bonds to them are drawn.
- The privacy scan of the publication history now reads up to 5,000 past files. The history had passed the earlier bound of 1,000, which left 13 unread and correctly stopped this publication.

## Limits

- The two levels above each molecule are simulated illustrations. Their sizes, counts and positions are estimates.
- Thyroglobulin is of the cow. The ribosome is from a mouse testis and differs from those of other cells in holding RPL39L. Claudin-15 is one of a family, and the bladder uses others. None of these was measured in the Digimouse specimen.
- No run on a graphics card has measured how smoothly the ribosome turns. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
