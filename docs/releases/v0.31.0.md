# v0.31.0 one unbroken zoom, a moving mouse and a plain view

The living mouse model now opens as it is zoomed, goes on down to atoms without a change of view, and moves. Its anatomy is unchanged: the GLB is byte-identical to that of v0.30.0, with 377 structures. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- [Zooming through the layers](../zoom-unfold.md). Scrolling towards any spot of the outside of the animal opens that spot, layer by layer, in the order in which the structures lie there in the model, and scrolling back closes them. The opening at one named place, formerly the main way in, remains as **Open a named place** in the **Layers** tab.
- [The zoom below the layers](../scale-journey.md). The same scroll goes on in the same place on the screen, without a separate view, into the tissue of the structure under the pointer, its cells and what they hold, down to the quarks of a proton. **Play** goes the whole way by itself. **Zoom from skin to atoms** is removed, because it is now the same route.
- Every one of the 377 structures has a route. New tissues are cartilage, tendon, serous membrane, gland, endocrine gland, thyroid, lymphoid tissue, colon, stomach, urinary lining, airway, retina, cornea, lens, tooth, testis and blood.
- Ways down other than a cell nucleus. Pointing at a red cell leads to the inside of the cell and to measured atoms of mouse hemoglobin, Protein Data Bank entry 3HRW. Pointing at the bacteria of the small intestine, caecum or colon leads to the inside of a bacterium and its DNA. These are simulated and marked as such, apart from the measured atoms.
- Any level can be looked around: dragging turns it, dragging with shift or the right button moves across it, and the zoom follows the pointer.
- [Illustrative motion](../living-motion.md). The heart beats, the chest and abdomen breathe, tokens travel along the simulated vessels and nerves, the gut moves in slow waves, and the head, tail, ears and whiskers move. Many levels below the layers move on the same clock. The rates of the heart, breathing, sniffing and whisking follow published values, and the rest is chosen for viewing and listed as such.
- The mouse answers: its head follows the pointer, a click on the closed animal makes it start, and **Walk** makes it step on the spot.
- A plain working view. Explanations, sources and evidence are out of the way of the mouse, and remain in the inspector, the **Sources** tab and control, the information button and the documents. Simulated structures keep a short `sim` beside their names, and motion stays named as illustrative.

## Limits

The motion is an illustration. It computes nothing about blood, gas, nerve signals or mechanics, and its tokens are far larger and slower than what they stand for. The stepping bends no joint and follows no measured gait. Everything below about 0.1 mm is representative and is not from this mouse, and some routes are the nearest kind of tissue and not an exact match. These are listed in the documents. Software rendering in a headless browser is far slower than the motion is meant to run, so its smoothness on a graphics card has not been measured, and nothing here has been reviewed by an anatomist, physiologist or microbiologist.
