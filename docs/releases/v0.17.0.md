# v0.17.0 living mouse model

This release adds a constructed static model of a whole mouse, and the application now opens on it. It combines measured data from one Digimouse specimen with simulated structures in a simulated standing posture. It is not a measurement of a living mouse. Every structure declares whether it is source-labelled, traced from the source images or simulated, and the interface shows that declaration wherever the structure appears. [Living mouse methods](../living-mouse.md) record the inputs, rules, sources and limits.

## Implemented behavior

- The living mouse reference holds 68 structures: 18 source-labelled surfaces, 7 traced from the coregistered source images, and 43 simulated. It is kept in its own catalog, apart from the source references, which are unchanged. Any reference can be opened directly with `?reference=<identifier>`.
- Traced from the images: the skeleton including fore paw digits from CT, intestines, skeletal muscle, adipose tissue, blood-filled vessels, air spaces and the body surface.
- Simulated from published descriptions: the coat, whiskers, ears, paws, claws, tail, foot and tail bones, aorta and named arteries, venae cavae and named veins, spinal cord, spinal and limb nerves, vagus, phrenic and cranial nerves, trachea, diaphragm, oesophagus, tongue, salivary glands, gall bladder, ureters, male ducts and accessory glands, thyroid, thymus, pituitary and lymph nodes.
- Each structure shows a Source label, Traced or Simulated mark in the list and details panel, with its basis. Labels on the model carry the same word. **Show by evidence** in Layers hides or shows a whole class, and a caption on the viewer states that the model is constructed.
- **Coat** in Display controls chooses an agouti, black or albino simulated coat, or none.
- Limb segments rotate about joints read from the CT, the rump is tucked and the head lifted. The source references keep their scanned posture.
- Publication checks reject a simulated structure that does not say so in its own description, and any structure without a declared class and basis.

## Executed verification

446 JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips, including new cases for surface extraction on known shapes, the posture rig, the tissue rules on values sampled from the images, the manifest and geometry binding, the evidence marks, the coat and the default reference. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The Python publication, registered-anatomy, launcher, release, distribution and resource suites passed.

A headless Chrome session with software WebGL rendered the model from the front, side, rear and below, with and without the coat, and with the arterial and venous, nervous, digestive, reproductive, skeletal and muscular layers isolated, without console errors. Screenshots were inspected visually. This is software rendering, not a measurement of performance on a real GPU, and it is not anatomical review.

## Scientific and operational limits

The posture, coat and all structures marked simulated are estimates and are not measured. Nerves follow a description of the rat, because the mouse sources used state that they do not describe the nervous system. Traced classes come from colour and density rules that have not been reviewed, and they contain errors at tissue boundaries. The muscle layer is not yet divided into named muscles. The frame has no accepted registration to the other references. Physical calibration, anatomical orientation and laterality remain unverified, and no part of the model has had independent anatomical review. The download grows by 47 MB.
