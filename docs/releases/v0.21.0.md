# v0.21.0 a more lifelike exterior

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 177 structures. It changes how the model looks from outside. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- The stance is higher. The ground lies 7.2 mm below the scanned table, the thigh is held nearly level, the knee stays high against the flank and the ankle comes to lie under the hip. The head is lifted by 16 degrees, and the bend of the rump is spread over a longer part of the back.
- The forearms are simulated tapered shapes, as the shanks already were, because the scanned forearms end in a blunt mass of tissue round the wrists.
- The coat has a stored pattern: a darker back, a paler belly with a diffuse border low on the flank, and hair that shortens and turns pale towards the paws. Neighbouring hairs lean together in small tufts, and short hair shows no dark root. The body carries about 367,000 finer hairs, up from about 280,000.
- Finer hair grows on the ears, the backs of the paws and, sparsely, the tail.
- The ears, tail, paws and nose take a colour that goes with the coat: greyish on an agouti mouse, dark on a black mouse and pink on an albino mouse. The skin seen between hairs is pale on the belly.
- The pinnae are reshaped after Cook figures 4 and 5, with a dark opening low on the front of a deeper bowl. This also corrects an error in earlier versions, in which the shading of the bowl was drawn on the back of the ear.
- The nose pad has nostrils and a groove down its middle. A new simulated structure, the mouth line, follows the lip as drawn in Cook figure 5.
- The eyelids lie on the globe as an almond-shaped line, and the whiskers taper, curve back along the face and fan about the horizontal.

## Executed verification

All JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips, including new cases for the coat pattern, hair length, fine hair, colours by coat and the stance. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The distribution, publication and release suites passed. A rebuild from the raw volumes was byte-identical. A headless Chrome session with software WebGL rendered the default view, the other side, the underside and top, the head at close range, the bare skin, and the agouti, black and albino coats, without console errors. This is software rendering and not anatomical review.

## Scientific and operational limits

The stance, the coat pattern, every colour and the shapes named above are estimates chosen by eye after published drawings and general knowledge of the wild-type pattern. They are not measurements of this specimen, which is a nude mouse, or of any other animal. The drawing of about 386,000 hairs was not timed on a real graphics processor; on a machine that draws slowly, fewer hairs are drawn while the view moves. All limits of the earlier living mouse releases still apply.
