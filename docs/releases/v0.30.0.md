# v0.30.0 paws, tail and nerves of the skin

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 377 structures: 36 source-labelled, 152 traced and 189 simulated. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- New simulated structures are the arteries, veins and nerves of the paws and digits. They are simple courses, one of each along each digit, and they are not divided into named branches.
- [All layers together](../all-layers.md) now opens the fore paw, hind paw and tail directly.
- The block of skin in the [zoom from skin to the proton](../scale-journey.md) now shows a nerve in the dermis, with a branch that ends round the neck of each follicle and fine endings towards the epidermis, as reviewed by Zimmerman et al. (2014).

## Limits

These structures are simulated, marked as such, and have not been compared with any dissection. The bones of the hind foot beyond the scan are simple shafts, so an opened hind paw shows less than a real one would. The tail holds only its vertebrae and the middle caudal vessels, and has no muscles or nerves.
