# v0.27.0 muscles of the eye, calcaneal tendons and traced fragments

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 299 structures: 36 source-labelled, 152 traced and 111 simulated. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- New simulated structures are the muscles of the eye and the calcaneal (Achilles) tendons.
- Marques et al. (2007) name rectus, oblique and retractor bulbi muscles dissected from the mouse, and Stuelsatz and Yablonka-Reuveni (2016) describe the primary and accessory muscles of the mouse eye. The model draws four recti and two obliques, the usual mammalian numbers, which were not confirmed from those papers, whose abstracts alone were read. Courses and sizes are estimates.
- Sakabe et al. (2018) work on the Achilles tendon of adult mice. The tendon is drawn from the gastrocnemius to the heel, along a course that is an estimate.

## Corrections

Three pieces traced as gut were not gut and have been removed: one of about 650 voxels in the forelimb as it lay in the scan, one of about 420 voxels in the left thigh, and one of about 1,900 voxels on the ventral midline below the bladder, where the colon does not run. They had the colours of gut contents and lay within the coarse mask of the abdominal cavity. The gut now holds only pieces of more than 6,000 voxels once grown, which are the main mass and the rectum, so the traced colon is shorter than before.

## A limitation found

Vertices of each structure were compared with the skin in the standing pose. Traced muscles and some bones of the limbs reach beyond the simulated limb skin. The soleus reaches about 2.8 mm beyond it, the forearm extensors about 2.6 mm, and the right tibia about 0.9 mm. About 1 percent of the vertices of the small intestine also lie beyond the skin at the right flank, by up to about 1.2 mm. The scanned limbs lay pressed flat, whereas the limb skin is a slender simulated shape, so the two do not fit. These structures are hidden while the skin is opaque, and a translucent skin can show them outside it. This has not been corrected. The comparison is a measure on vertices, is not part of the build, and is not exact where surfaces of the paws, tail and eyes close the body instead of the skin.

## Limits

These structures are simulated, marked as such, and have not been compared with any dissection.
