# v0.36.0 the whisker and the class I molecule

The zoom gains a route of its own into the whiskers, and two more molecules of the mouse, so that twenty-three entries of the Protein Data Bank now supply its atoms. The source references and the anatomical model are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- **A route into a whisker:** a follicle cut along its length, then the Merkel cells of its outer sheath, after Ebara et al. 2002 ([doi:10.1002/cne.10277](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.10277)), who describe the rat and the cat. It serves the whiskers and their follicles, which used to go by the skin.
- **A way to mouse Piezo2** ([6KG7](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6KG7)): a Merkel cell, the domes in its membrane, and the measured channel with its three blades. After Woo et al. 2014 ([doi:10.1038/nature13251](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13251)) and Wang et al. 2019 ([doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1505-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1505-8)). **Play** from a whisker takes this way.
- **A way to a mouse class I molecule** ([2VAA](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2VAA)) from lymphoid tissue, if chosen, by the surface of a lymphocyte, with the atoms of the peptide it holds. After Fremont et al. 1992 ([doi:10.1126/science.1323877](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1323877)).

## Limits

- The levels above each molecule are simulated illustrations, with estimated sizes, counts and positions. The follicle follows descriptions of other species.
- Parts of the blades of Piezo2 could not be modelled and are absent. The class I molecule has no part in the membrane and holds a peptide of a virus, placed there for the crystal.
- Neither molecule was measured in the Digimouse specimen. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
