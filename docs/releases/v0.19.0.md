# v0.19.0 named bones, branching trees and membranes

This release adds further layers to the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md) and makes it lighter. It now holds 170 structures in 39 MB, against 124 structures in 68 MB before. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- The skeleton is divided into 24 named bones and groups of bones, from the skull and vertebral regions to the limb bones. Each limb bone moves as one rigid piece about its joint. CT bone outside the labelled body is now accepted only at the fore paws, which removes fragments of the scanning holder near the snout.
- Simulated branching trees fill the source organ labels and traced gut: bronchi, pulmonary arteries and veins, hepatic artery, portal and hepatic vein branches, renal arteries and veins, cerebral arteries, and mesenteric arteries and veins. They show what lies in each organ, but no branch follows a real vessel or airway.
- New simulated structures are the basilar artery and arterial circle at the base of the brain, the thoracic duct and lymph trunks, eyelids, and faint membranes: pericardium, pleura, meninges and peritoneum.
- The source label "rest of the brain" is divided by estimate into the basal forebrain, thalamus, hypothalamus, hippocampus, midbrain and pons.
- Ears, paws and tail carry tints: a darker concha and fine radial lines on the ear, darker walking pads, and rings on the tail.
- Slow machines draw fewer and wider hairs while the view moves, and the full coat returns when it stops. The Layers tab opens first on this reference.

## Executed verification

453 JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips, including new cases for tree growth, the branching law, the assignment of bones, and the thinning of hairs, which still covers the whole body. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The distribution, publication and release suites passed.

A headless Chrome session with software WebGL rendered the model from the front, top and below, with the coat and with skin only, and the skeleton, blood vessel, muscle and organ steps, without console errors. Screenshots were inspected visually. This is software rendering, not a measurement of performance on a real GPU, and it is not anatomical review. The thinning of hairs in motion has not been observed on a real GPU.

## Scientific and operational limits

Trees are generated by a rule and are not anatomical courses. Bone and brain boundaries are estimates, and vertebrae of one region and individual ribs are not separated. Membrane positions are estimates and carry no measured thickness. All limits of [v0.17.0](v0.17.0.md) and [v0.18.0](v0.18.0.md) still apply.
