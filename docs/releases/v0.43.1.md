# v0.43.1 nothing hangs below the paws

A release that corrects the paws of the living mouse after a researcher noticed muscle-coloured tissue under the feet in the Muscles layer. The paws themselves, the measured bones and every other structure keep their shape. This version is published on the main branch without packaged release archives; v0.41.1 remains the latest packaged release.

## Implemented behavior

- **Tissue below the paws:** traced tissue that lay below the soles, up to 2.1 mm below them, is gone. The build kept soft tissue under the skin everywhere except below the wrists and ankles, where it expected the simulated paws to hold it; the traced tissue of the scanned paws, which lay flattened in the scan, did not fit inside them. That tissue, 5,923 of the 45,572 triangles of other muscle tissue, is now left out, and its description says so.
- **Vessels and nerves at the paws:** a few points of the radial and median arteries and of the median and ulnar nerves that stood outside the paws are drawn in just under their surface.
- **A check in the build:** below the wrists and ankles, soft tissue more than half a millimetre outside the paws, the body and the limbs now stops the build, as tissue outside the body already did elsewhere.
- **Honest descriptions of the paw bones:** the paws are sculpted rather than fitted to the measured paw bones, and parts of those bones lie outside them, by up to 1.1 mm for the right fore paw, 2.1 mm for the left fore paw, 1.8 mm for the right ankle and 0.8 mm for the left ankle. The fore paws were described as holding their digit bones; the descriptions of the paws and of the paw bones now give these figures. The bones are not moved, because they are measured, and the paws were not reshaped around them, because doing so left them swollen and lumpy.

## Limits

- The sculpted paws still do not enclose all of the measured paw bones, by the amounts above. Fitting the paws to the bones would mean sculpting them again.
- The living mouse model is now 50,786,180 bytes, with 2,325,169 triangles. The model and its other limits are recorded in [living mouse methods](../living-mouse.md) and [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
