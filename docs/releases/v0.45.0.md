# v0.45.0 paws sculpted round their bones

A release that sculpts all four paws of the living mouse round the measured bones of the scanned paws, takes out of the animal the bed and the other objects that the CT shows beside it, gives the heel of the left foot back to the foot, keeps the wall of each flank with the trunk when the thigh turns, keeps the cheek and neck with the head where a forearm lay against them, joins the simulated limbs to the body without a ragged line, makes the skin and the paws closed surfaces, and lights the animal alike from every side. It replaces v0.44.0, which was not published: its fore paws followed the surface of the bones too closely, and the left one showed a seam along the palm and moulded digits. On 2026-11-13, while the paws were reworked, the public site was returned to v0.43.0 at the maintainer's choice; this version contains the correction of [v0.43.1](v0.43.1.md), which took the traced tissue from below the paws. No packaged release archives are made for this version; v0.41.1 remains the latest packaged release.

## Implemented behavior

- **Fore paws sculpted round their bones:** in v0.44.0 each fore paw was a layer of 0.2 mm drawn over the surface of the bones of the scanned paw, so that it showed every ridge of that surface, and the joins between its parts showed as seams. Each fore paw is now sculpted round the same bones. The palm is a rounded shape that holds the bones of the palm, the start of each digit and the ends of the forearm bones within 2.2 mm of the wrist, drawn 0.3 mm beyond them and smoothed. Each digit is a tube along the middle of its bones, as wide as they need with 0.15 mm of soft tissue over them, with no bulges along it, joined smoothly to the palm and by a short web to its neighbours. Walking pads after Cook figure 6 lie under the tips of the digits, between their starts and at the base of the palm, claws grow from the ends of the digits in the CT, and each pollex carries a nail. Every vertex of the bones lies under the surface of its paw, by at least 0.03 mm, where in v0.43.1 parts of them stood up to 2.3 mm outside.
- **An object beside the left fore paw:** the short piece beside the fifth digit of the left fore paw, which v0.44.0 drew as an outer digit without a claw, is the edge of an object beside the animal: at the level of soft tissue nothing in the CT joins it to the paw. Its 300 voxels are left out, and the left fore paw has four digits and a pollex, as the right has.
- **The bed and other objects beside the animal:** the CT shows objects whose edges are as bright as bone within 2.5 mm of the labelled body: the bed under the animal, and upright pieces beside the left fore paw and the right knee. Bright CT outside the labelled body is now taken for the animal only where tissue joins it to the labelled body and where its piece lies in a paw or touches the body with at least one voxel in twenty. By this rule 5,816 voxels that no tissue joins to the body and 5,053 in 17 pieces that only graze it are left out. Among them is an upright piece of 1,674 voxels beside the right knee, which earlier versions took for part of the animal and drew at the right hind limb.
- **The wall of the flank stays with the trunk:** in the scan the thighs lie spread against the flanks, and the nearest bone to the wall of the flank in front of a knee is the femur, as far as 12.9 mm from it. That wall therefore turned forwards with the thigh and left a groove along the flank, deeper on the right, which showed as a dark band from the right side. A thigh now carries only the tissue within 6 mm of its femur at the hip, falling to 3.4 mm at the knee, an estimate; the tissue beyond, 33 percent of the soft tissue nearest the right femur and 38 percent of that nearest the left, stays with the trunk. Both flanks now have the same form.
- **The cheek and neck stay with the head:** in the scan the forearms lie stretched forwards against the head and the neck. The smoothed weights that carry tissue into the stance let the tissue of the cheek and neck that touched a forearm follow it part of the way, as far as 13 mm, which left a knob of skin about 3 mm long under the left side of the neck, present in the published v0.43.0 as well, and they held the tissue of the forearm back. A forearm and a paw are free of the trunk and the head in the animal, so where their weights meet, the stronger side now keeps the tissue whole and the other takes no part in its movement. The knob is gone, and the tissue of the forearm is no longer held back. Traced fat is not drawn within 0.45 mm of such a parting, where it joined two parts that only touched; 17,416 voxels of it are left out. The build records every triangle with corners on both sides of a parting and stops if one is stretched by more than 0.5 mm; the most is 0.12 mm.
- **Limbs joined to the body without a ragged line:** where a simulated shank or forearm met the body, the grid of 0.3 mm drew the line between them as a row of teeth, and at the right knee as a ragged pit. The surface is now smoothed once more where a limb meets the body, over 0.6 mm, and the limbs join the body in a smooth fold.
- **Tissue follows the bones as drawn:** each skeleton voxel now holds the tissue round it to the limb segment that its bone, as drawn, moves with. Before, a voxel of a limb bone more than 2 mm from the straight line between its joints, as round the knees, held its tissue to the trunk.
- **The heel of the left foot:** in the scan the left foot lies across the end of its shank, with the heel on one side of the ankle and the toes on the other. v0.44.0 gave the bone of the heel to the shank, 3,595 voxels of it, and carried it into the stance 1.2 mm outside the shank. A shank is now taken to end at the plane through its ankle across its line, and the bone beyond that plane is bone of the foot; on the right this moves 184 voxels. The turns that lay the bones of each ankle along the foot were found again from the bones as now divided, and differ by 1.7 degrees on the right and 1.1 on the left; the ankles stand 0.03 and 0.02 mm lower.
- **Hind paws:** the heel of each hind paw holds the bones of the ankle, and the ends of the shank bones within 1 mm of it, in rounded shapes of the same kind as the palms, where a thin layer over them showed as lumps. The ankle rises along the line of the shank, where it rose straight up beside it.
- **Closed surfaces:** where two sheets of a surface drawn from a field would meet along an edge, they are now parted. The skin of the body had 28 edges shared by four triangles in v0.43.1 and 40 in v0.44.0, the nose pad 3, and the hind paws of v0.43.1 were open at the top; the skin, each paw, the tail and the nose pad are now closed surfaces whose every edge lies between two triangles, and the build stops if the skin or a paw is not, if a paw has a tunnel through it, or if a paw leaves outside it a vertex of the bones it is drawn round.
- **The tail meets the ground in a curve:** where the tail came down to the ground it bent sharply. It now falls ever less steeply as it nears the ground and meets it without a bend.
- **Lights that turn with the view:** the lights stood still, so that the default view showed the near side of the animal in soft shade, with its form, and a view from the other side showed that side lit from the front, flat, with every unevenness of the coat picked out. The lights, the environment and the hemisphere light now turn with the view about the dorsal direction and stand as before in the default view, which is unchanged pixel for pixel. While the mouse walks they turn with the view of it, and at the dissection bench they follow the eye as before.

## Limits

- The soft tissue, pads, claws and nails of the paws are simulated. The room left for soft tissue over the bones, the sizes of the pads, the 0.3 mm under the bones, the turns of the paws, the heights of the wrists and ankles and the reach of the thighs are estimates and choices, not measurements of a standing animal.
- At the resolution of the CT the small bones of a paw are not separated from each other or wholly from the soft tissue round them, the joint spaces of the wrists and ankles do not show, and the hind feet beyond the ankle are not in the images.
- Where a measured bone comes within 0.1 mm of the skin, the skin is drawn out over it, by up to 0.33 mm, over the tip of the os penis.
- Carrying the scanned tissue into the stance still stretches some triangles to more than 1 mm: the longest edge is 4.4 mm, in the fat between the left shank and the root of the tail. A shank is not parted from the trunk as a forearm is, because the skin of the thigh and the flank joins them, so the tissue between a shank and the rump is still drawn out with the shank.
- Where a forearm lay against the neck in the scan, the skin of the neck keeps the shallow folds it had there. Traced fat that lay round a scanned forearm or shank, outside the slender simulated limb, is drawn in under the skin of the limb and lies folded there. Seen from behind, the pale band of the hairs shows in rows across the nape.
- The checks of this version are those of one browser on one machine, and the model has not been reviewed by an anatomist. They are recorded in [acceptance](../acceptance.md), and the model in [living mouse methods](../living-mouse.md).
