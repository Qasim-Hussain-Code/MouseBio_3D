# v0.24.0 zoom from skin to the proton

This release adds [a separate view](../scale-journey.md) that continues the zoom beyond the data of the specimen, from a block of skin to the inside of a proton. The anatomical references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- **Zoom from skin to atoms** opens the view, and so does zooming on to the skin of the standing animal in the main view while the skin is visible and no section is drawn. Zooming back out past the block of skin, the Escape key and **Back to the mouse** all return to the mouse.
- Scrolling passes without a break through eleven levels: a block of haired skin, the cells of the epidermis, one keratinocyte, the cell nucleus, a chromatin fibre, one mouse nucleosome, the atoms of its DNA, a carbon atom, the inside of the atom, the nucleus of carbon-12 and a proton. A list of the levels moves directly to any of them, and dragging turns the view.
- Each level shows its kind of evidence: simulated from published descriptions, measured in a crystal, calculated from a model of the atom, or schematic. A notice at every level states that data from this specimen end at about 0.1 mm and that the view was not seen in this mouse. The sources of each level open beside its caption.
- The two crystal levels draw the 11,986 atoms of Protein Data Bank entry 5B1M at their deposited positions. If that file cannot be read or is not valid, they stay empty and say so.
- A scale bar drawn to true length and the height of the view are shown at every level.

## Limits

Nothing in this view comes from the specimen, and it adds nothing to any anatomical reference. Its simulated levels are generated illustrations of published descriptions, with estimated sizes and counts. The crystal is of a purified particle whose DNA the entry records as human in origin. The electron density uses Slater orbitals, which are an approximation, and the two deepest levels are schematic drawings, since nucleons and quarks have no fixed places and no size has been measured for a quark. Nobody has reviewed the view independently. See [the description of the view](../scale-journey.md) for what each level rests on.

## Planned

Branches from the single line of descent, such as the cell membrane as a bilayer, a keratin filament or a ribosome from further entries of the Protein Data Bank, have not been built.
