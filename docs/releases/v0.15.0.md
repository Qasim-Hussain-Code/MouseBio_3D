# v0.15.0 specimen appearance and added layers

This release shows the source specimens as hairless mice with their dorsal side up, removes the dark viewer background, and adds anatomy: a finer skeleton surface for the registered-volume reference and a separate micro-CT reference whose organ classes include the intestine. [The prototype guide](../prototype-guide.md) describes the complete current application; [viewer presentation methods](../viewer-presentation.md) and [micro-CT methods](../microct-geometry.md) record the conventions and sources introduced here.

## Implemented behavior

- The viewer has a single white background. View files from earlier versions that record the dark background, or none, load with the white background after the usual source-binding checks; new files record white.
- In both Digimouse references the body envelope opens as opaque pink skin, matching the hairless nude specimen, with a **Skin** opacity control in the viewer. Selecting a region inside the body, or opening Living, Experiment or Compare, makes opaque skin see-through; labels of regions hidden inside opaque skin are not shown. Isolating a translucent region shows it opaque, and Restore returns its previous opacity.
- The Digimouse references and the micro-CT reference are displayed with their dorsal side up above a soft ground shadow, and the orbit turns about the dorsal direction. The dorsal direction is inferred from the positions of each reference's own skeleton, kidneys, heart and bladder labels and documented as an inference; laterality, calibration and the source axes remain unverified.
- The aggregate skeleton of the registered-volume reference has an optional 0.2 mm regional surface (1,016,548 triangles, 24,383,356 bytes) from the existing reviewed converter, loaded on request like the other finer surfaces.
- A separate micro-CT reference adapts one native in vivo scan of a female BALB/c nu/nu mouse from Rosenhain et al. (2018), whose figshare dataset record declares CC0 1.0: the body and the ten classes of the first segmentation file, namely bone, lungs, heart, liver, intestine, bladder, spleen, stomach, part of a thigh muscle and kidneys, on the original 0.28 mm grid. Only the scan's members were read from the 4.5 GB archive by range requests and bound to checksums. Agreement with the scan's second segmentation file is reported per class. The scanned field covers the trunk, so its body opens see-through. It has its own identifiers, frame, source groups and inferred posture, and no registration to Digimouse.
- Voxel-derived display surfaces receive diffused vertex normals after the Taubin filter, so that long terraces no longer show in the shading; the passes per surface are limited by its thickness, and vertex positions are unchanged.

## Executed verification

416 JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips on the final committed source, including new cases for the white-only view state and its migrations, the skin control and its see-through rules, the inferred postures on the shipped surfaces, the ground shadow in two orientations, the micro-CT manifest, catalog, groups, frame, posture and smoothing departures, the skeleton detail inventory and the normal diffusion. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The Python geometry, publication, registered-anatomy, launcher, release, distribution and resource suites passed, including a new micro-CT geometry suite that reproduces the asset from the extracted source members with an identical SHA-256; the Digimouse volume asset and the 0.2 mm heart and skeleton surfaces were regenerated with identical checksums after the converter was generalized. The staged publication scan reported no errors or warnings with 30 registered anatomy assets.

Headless Chrome sessions with software WebGL rendered the white interface, the skin and posture of both Digimouse references, the see-through volume organs, the 0.2 mm skeleton and the micro-CT organs with ten placed labels, without console errors. On this machine, which had less than 1 GB of free memory during the sessions, worker throughput in the headless browser varied widely: the same 21-surface smoothing batch completed in 9 seconds in one probe and stalled for more than 10 minutes inside the application in another, and reference loads took from about 2 to more than 25 minutes. These timings are not representative of an interactive browser; details are recorded in [acceptance criteria](../acceptance.md).

## Scientific and operational limits

The skin colour, posture, ground shadow and diffused normals are presentation conventions; the dorsal inferences do not verify the source axes or laterality. The micro-CT specimen is one athymic, hairless female whose age and mass are not reported; its two segmentation files disagree for bone and spleen, its body class is identified by inference, and its scanned field truncates the head, limbs and tail. Whole-body vessels, nerves, glands and most muscles remain absent from the bundled whole-body references. The registered anatomy total rose to about 182 MB, and the publication, packaging and launcher bounds were raised to 256 MiB. Representative hardware measurement, keyboard and assistive-technology review, and independent anatomical and physiological review remain outstanding.
