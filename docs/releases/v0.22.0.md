# v0.22.0 individual bones

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 256 structures, of which 99 are bones or groups of bones traced from the CT. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- The skeleton is divided bone by bone: 12 bones and paired bones of the skull, both mandibles, 7 cervical, 13 thoracic, 6 lumbar and 4 sacral vertebrae, 5 caudal vertebrae in the scan, 26 ribs, 6 parts of the sternum, and separate clavicles, scapulae and ossa coxae. Limb bones are as before. Radius and ulna, tibia and fibula, and the bones of each paw are still not separated.
- Vertebrae, ribs, sternum and scapulae are divided by the shortest path through bone from seeds, with limits read from this specimen's CT. The counts agree with Hummel et al. The bones of the skull are divided by position after Cook figures 11 to 13, because the CT shows no sutures. Every one of these divisions is marked as estimated.
- **Colours** in Display controls has a new choice, **Contrasting colours**, which tells neighbouring structures apart. Its colours carry no meaning.
- New simulated structures are the intervertebral discs, the xiphoid cartilage, and intercostal arteries and veins. The vessels follow the course of each rib measured in the CT, offset by an estimate.
- The simulated tail now holds 23 vertebral shafts, which with the 5 caudal vertebrae in the CT make 28, within the range counted by Hummel et al.

## Corrections

- The azygos vein was drawn on the right. It now runs on the left of the aorta and joins the left superior vena cava, as in Cook figure 95.
- The pinnae were placed too high. The CT shows the external auditory meatus, and they now lie lower on the side of the head, above it.

## Executed verification

All JavaScript and simulated DOM cases passed with no failures or skips, including new cases for the list of bones and its counts, division by position, growth through bone, rib courses and contrasting colours. TypeScript, dependency notices and the production build passed. The distribution, publication and release suites passed. A rebuild from the raw volumes was byte-identical. A headless Chrome session with software WebGL rendered the coat, the skeleton in tissue colours and in contrasting colours from several sides, and the blood vessels, without console errors. This is software rendering and not anatomical review.

## Scientific and operational limits

The names and limits of bones are estimates, and nobody has checked them against the specimen by dissection or by higher-resolution imaging. In the neck and sacrum the CT shows the limits between vertebrae less clearly, and where false ribs run together their ventral segments may be assigned to the wrong rib. Skull divisions come from a drawing of another mouse scaled to this skull. Discs are not visible in this CT. All limits of the earlier living mouse releases still apply.
