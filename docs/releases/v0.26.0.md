# v0.26.0 nose, throat, salivary glands and a check on placement

This release continues the [living mouse model](../living-mouse.md), which now holds 297 structures: 36 source-labelled, 152 traced and 109 simulated. The source references are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- New simulated structures follow Hummel et al.: the nasal cavities and nasopharynx, the dorsal and lateral turbinals, the soft palate and the pharynx.
- The single salivary structure is now three pairs, as Hummel et al. describe them: large compact submandibular glands in the ventral neck, small flattened sublingual glands pressed against their ventral anterolateral surfaces, and diffuse lobulated parotid glands from the ears to the clavicles. The earlier sublingual gland lay behind the submandibular gland, on the wrong side of it.
- The build now stops if more than 0.1 percent of the vertices of any simulated interior lie over half a millimetre outside the labelled body of the specimen.

## Corrections

Measuring every simulated interior against the labelled body of the specimen, in the scanned frame, found that some lay outside it. These parts of earlier versions were wrong and have moved under the surface:

| Structure | Vertices outside the body before | Vertices outside the body now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Veins of the neck, in the superior venae cavae and jugular structure | 14.8 percent, 10.4 percent by over 0.5 mm | 0.2 percent |
| Salivary glands | 17.7 percent, 3.5 percent by over 0.5 mm | None |
| Lymph nodes | 6.4 percent, 3.7 percent by over 0.5 mm | None |
| Spinal nerves | 1.5 percent, 0.9 percent by over 0.5 mm | None |
| Lymph trunks | 2.2 percent | None |
| Preputial glands | 12.3 percent | 0.8 percent |
| Penis | 5.6 percent | 2.0 percent |
| Ear canals | 1.3 percent, 0.3 percent by over 0.5 mm | 0.5 percent |

What remains outside lies within half a millimetre of the surface, at the external auditory meatus and at the ventral surface near the penis. The measure applies to the scanned frame and does not by itself show that nothing crosses the skin in the simulated stance.

The first version of the [coverage list](../living-mouse-coverage.md) named costal cartilages as missing. Hummel et al. describe the ventral segments of the ribs as calcified, and in the model ribs 2 to 7 reach the sternum, so the list now says that instead.

## Evidence for placement

The bony palate was read from the median section of the CT and from colour sections, and ends near y 208. The size of the nasal cavities at each level was read from dark passages in colour sections. The CT was tried first, but its low-density voxels cover only small patches of the passages, so they could not be traced, and the shape is an estimate. The turbinals are too fine to separate at this resolution and are simple ridges.

## Limits

These structures are simulated, marked as such, and have not been compared with any dissection. The separate parts of the small intestine remain undone, because its coils cannot be followed in the images.
