# v0.37.0 the pancreas and insulin

The zoom gains a route of its own into the pancreas and a way to insulin, so that twenty-four entries of the Protein Data Bank now supply its atoms. The source references and the anatomical model are unchanged.

## Implemented behavior

- **A route into the pancreas:** a block of acini and ducts with an islet among them, then the cells of the islet, after Palade 1975 ([doi:10.1126/science.1096303](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1096303)) and Cabrera et al. 2006 ([doi:10.1073/pnas.0510790103](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0510790103)). The pancreas used to go by the general route of a gland.
- **A way to the insulin hexamer** ([4INS](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4INS)): a beta cell, a granule with its crystal of hexamers, the measured hexamer, and the atoms round its zinc. After Baker et al. 1988 ([doi:10.1098/rstb.1988.0058](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.1988.0058)) and Dodson and Steiner 1998 ([doi:10.1016/s0959-440x(98)80037-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0959-440x(98)80037-7)). **Play** from the pancreas takes this way.
- A name is no longer shown twice in the atoms when an element is also the group that holds the view.

## Limits

- The levels above the molecule are simulated illustrations, with estimated sizes, counts and positions. The share of islets in the block is not measured, and the order of the crystal in a real granule is drawn.
- This insulin is of the pig and not of the mouse, and the mouse has two insulin genes of its own. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
