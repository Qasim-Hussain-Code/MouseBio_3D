# v0.41.0 the kidney found in every demonstration, a finer eye and sources from the details

A small release that mends the one demonstration that could stop short in v0.40.0, refines the eye, and links the details of a structure to the page of sources.

## Implemented behavior

- **Finding a kidney:** when the hands demonstrate finding the organs of a necropsy, they now move the intestine 15 mm aside instead of 12 mm and part four strips of fat over the right kidney instead of three. In a wide window the kidney had stayed hidden in two of four demonstrations of v0.40.0, which left the step undone.
- **The eye:** the margin of each eyelid is drawn finer and the simulated cornea clearer, so that from outside the eye shows chiefly as a dark globe and its shine.
- **Sources from the details:** in the details of a structure, each work it rests on is a link where it can be followed, and a link opens the page of sources at that structure.

## Limits

- The cause of the hidden kidney was not found. A trace of the hands showed that they took hold of the intestine and moved it in the runs that failed as in those that passed, and the kidney showed with only about fifty pixels in view even when it was found. Moving the intestine further and parting more fat makes the kidney show more, and that is what was changed.
- All of this is practice on a simulated animal. It is not a protocol, and it does not replace supervised training under an approved protocol. See [acceptance](../acceptance.md).
