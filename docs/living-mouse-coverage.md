# What the living mouse model holds and leaves out

This list describes the static [living mouse model](living-mouse.md) of 385 structures: 36 source-labelled, 152 traced from the source images and 189 simulated. Being listed means that a structure is present in some form. It does not mean that the structure has been reviewed by an anatomist, which none has. A model of one male specimen cannot show what differs in other mice.

## Held by the model

| System | Structures | What is present | Parts held together, or estimated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skeleton | 108 | 103 bones traced from the CT by estimated divisions, with simulated intervertebral discs, xiphoid cartilage, patellae, fabellae and hind foot bones | Paired skull bones are not separated. Radius and ulna are one piece, because the CT does not separate them. Bones of the fore paws are not divided. Hummel et al. describe the ventral segments of the ribs as calcified, and the first seven pairs as attached to the sternum. In the model ribs 2 to 7 reach the sternum, and rib 1 stops about 2 mm short of it. |
| Muscle | 46 | Named muscles and groups, with estimated divisions of traced tissue, a simulated panniculus carnosus, simulated muscles of the eye and simulated calcaneal tendons | Many are groups of muscles. Traced muscles of the shanks and forearms were drawn in under the slender simulated limb skin, because the scanned limbs lay pressed flat. |
| Nervous | 52 | Eleven source-labelled parts of the brain, with simulated ventricles, meninges, spinal cord, ganglia, sympathetic trunks and separately named cranial, spinal and limb nerves | The roots and three branches of the sciatic nerve follow work in the mouse. Other nerves follow a description of the rat. |
| Cardiovascular | 74 | Source-labelled chambers of the heart, traced blood-filled vessels, simulated separately named arteries and veins, valves and trees within organs | The arteries of the hind limb and two veins of the thigh follow dissections in the mouse. The arteries of the forearm are not divided, because no source for the mouse was checked. The branches of simulated trees follow no real vessel. |
| Respiratory | 14 | Source-labelled lobes of the lungs, traced air spaces, simulated nasal cavities, turbinals, larynx, trachea, bronchial tree, diaphragm and pleura | The trachea of the specimen is collapsed and could not be followed. The nasal cavities have sizes read from colour sections, and the turbinals are simple ridges, because the CT does not resolve them. |
| Digestive | 25 | Source-labelled lobes of the liver, regions of the stomach and pancreas, traced intestines, simulated soft palate, pharynx, oesophagus, tongue, three pairs of salivary glands, ducts, teeth and peritoneum | The small intestine is one piece, because its coils cannot be followed in the images. |
| Urinary | 7 | Source-labelled kidneys and bladder, simulated ureters, urethra, medullae, pelves and lumen of the bladder | |
| Endocrine | 7 | Source-labelled adrenal glands, simulated medullae, parathyroids, pineal, thyroid, thymus and pituitary | |
| Reproductive | 8 | Source-labelled testes, simulated ducts, accessory glands and penis, with an os penis traced from the CT | Only the male tract. |
| Lymphatic | 13 | Source-labelled spleen, simulated lymph nodes at eleven sites, thoracic duct and lymph trunks | The size and place of each node are estimates. |
| Sensory | 10 | Source-labelled lachrymal glands, simulated eye layers, Harderian glands, ear canals, tympanic membranes, ossicles, cochleae and semicircular canals | Shapes are estimates. |
| Skin | 13 | Traced body surface and fat, simulated coat, vibrissae, eyelids, pinnae, paws, claws, tail and brown fat | The specimen is a nude mouse, so the coat is simulated. |

## Left out

These are known to exist and are not in the model. The list is not complete.

- **Skeleton and joints:** joint capsules, ligaments other than the patellar tendons, the articular cartilage of joints other than the hip, knee, ankle, shoulder, elbow and wrist, marrow outside the shafts of the femur, tibia and humerus, and the separate carpal, metacarpal and phalangeal bones of the fore paw.
- **Skull:** the ethmoid, vomer and lacrimal bones as separate bones.
- **Muscle and tendon:** tendons other than the calcaneal tendons, muscles of the tongue, pharynx, soft palate and paws, and the individual muscles now held in groups.
- **Gut:** the duodenum, jejunum and ileum as separate structures, and the wall layers of the gut.
- **Glands of the head and skin:** the gland of the ear canal.
- **Nerve and vessel:** autonomic plexuses, the division of the enteric plexus into its two layers, named vessels and nerves of the paws and digits, which are simple courses, and small vessels, nerves and lymphatics in general. No simulated tree follows a real vessel.
- **Other sexes and ages:** the female tract and mammary glands, because the specimen is an adult male.
- **Tissue and cell detail in the anatomical model:** there is none below about 0.1 mm. [The zoom below the layers](scale-journey.md) illustrates such scales for every structure, by way of thirty kinds of tissue, with [a measured molecule for most of them](scale-journey.md#a-molecule-for-each-tissue), and is not data from this specimen. A route is the nearest kind of tissue and not always an exact match.
