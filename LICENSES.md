The Digimouse source is attributed to Belma Dogdas, David Stout, Arion F. Chatziioannou and Richard M. Leahy, USC Biomedical Imaging Group and UCLA collaborators. Its source publication is [Dogdas et al. (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9155/52/3/003). Qasim Hussain's tetrahedral adaptation extracts source-region boundaries, orients faces, calculates display normals, converts to glTF and applies a uniform coordinate scale and nonquantitative colours. The registered-volume adaptation explicitly subsamples categorical labels, extracts binary half-value interfaces on a consistent tetrahedral grid, calculates outward winding and area-weighted normals, and encodes indexed glTF surfaces with nonquantitative colours. It supplies no additional source labels, laterality or registration between references. No endorsement by the source authors is implied.

The hindlimb source is Charles JP, Cappellari O, Spence AJ, Hutchinson JR and Wells DJ, [Musculoskeletal Geometry, Muscle Architecture and Functional Specialisations of the Mouse Hindlimb](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0147669), S1 Figure, 2016. The adaptation extracts the source surfaces from the figure's PRC stream, preserves the source labels and colours, applies the declared unit conversion, normalizes the existing vertex normals and encodes the result as glTF. Its standalone decoding tool has a separate AGPL-3.0-or-later license and is not bundled with the application. No registration to Digimouse or endorsement by the source authors is implied.

EMA147 is attributed to Seth Ruffins, Russell Jacobs, Chris Armit, Nick Burton, Bill Hill and Richard Baldock, University of Edinburgh, 2017, [doi:10.7488/ds/2167](https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2167). Its adaptation indexes exactly equal positions within each surface, stores native coordinates as float32 values and generates display normals from existing triangles. Source topology defects, annotation discrepancies and uncertain units remain explicit. No physical scale, anatomical repairs or cross-reference registration are introduced.
